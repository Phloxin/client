FFmpeg in Pylon's media engine (Linux)

The libraries in this folder (libavcodec, libavfilter, libavutil) are FFmpeg 9.0.2, licensed
under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later (COPYING.LGPLv2.1 in licenses/).
They were built without --enable-gpl and without --enable-nonfree. pylon-media-host links them
dynamically and loads them from this folder (its run path is $ORIGIN); they may be replaced by a
compatible build.

The build carries one patch, ffmpeg-vaapi-rate-change.patch (a changed bit rate, rate control
buffer or frame rate reaches an open VA-API encoder, and a `filler` option). The source is the
release tarball https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
(SHA-256 8c3850283eb25fa026482078a04051e0be17347b09ef81a0849bec15a96e002e) with that patch
applied. The patch, and the recipe that configures and builds it (linux/ffmpeg-build.sh), are in
the Pylon client's repository under native/media-engine/: https://github.com/Phloxin/client

Nothing else is linked into the libraries. libva, libdrm and the C library they use are the
system's own.
