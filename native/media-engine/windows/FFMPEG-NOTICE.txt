FFmpeg in Pylon's media engine (Windows)

The DLLs in this folder (avcodec, avfilter, avutil) are FFmpeg 9.0.2, licensed under the
GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later (COPYING.LGPLv2.1 in licenses\).
They were built without --enable-gpl and without --enable-nonfree. pylon-media-host.exe links
them dynamically and loads them from this folder; they may be replaced by a compatible build.

The build carries one patch, nvenc-reconfig-no-idr.patch (h264_nvenc's reconfig_idr option).
The source is the release tarball https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
(SHA-256 8c3850283eb25fa026482078a04051e0be17347b09ef81a0849bec15a96e002e) with that patch
applied. The patch, and the recipe that configures and builds it (windows/ffmpeg-build.sh and
windows/ffmpeg.md), are in the Pylon client's repository under native/media-engine/:
https://github.com/Phloxin/client

Linked statically into the DLLs, with their licence texts in licenses\: Intel's libvpl
dispatcher (MIT) and mingw-w64's winpthreads (MIT and BSD). The NVENC interface headers of
nv-codec-headers (MIT) are compiled in.
