Lucky Seven Flip cards and bank points. Repeat a number and you bust. First to 200 wins.
First to 200

    How to play

    • On your turn, Hit to flip a card or Stay to bank what you have.
    • Flip a number you already hold and you bust: nothing this round.
    • Collect seven different numbers and the round ends at once, with 15 extra points for you.
    • Your round score is your numbers added up, doubled by ×2, plus your bonus cards.
    • The game ends after a round with someone at 200 or more. The highest total wins.
