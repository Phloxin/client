Match the colour or the symbol. The first to play every card wins.

    How to play

    • On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the pile by colour or by symbol. A wild goes on anything.
    • Nothing to play, or you would rather not? Draw a card from the deck: you keep it and your turn ends.
    • The first player to play their last card wins the round.
    • A player who leaves keeps their seat until the round ends; the table draws for them.
